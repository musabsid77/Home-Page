# 📞 Contact Our Company Page
A well-structured HTML page designed to enable users to easily reach out to your business. This project presents a clear, accessible interface with a contact form, office hours, location details, and navigation links—all built using semantic HTML.

## 📋 Project Overview
The "Contact Our Company" webpage allows visitors to:
1. Navigate to key sections like Home, About, Services, and Contact.
2. Fill out a detailed contact form to send inquiries.
3. View the company’s office address and working hours.
4. Understand available communication channels and preferred methods.

## ✨ Features

* **Header & Navigation Menu**: Simple top-section that introduces the company and provides easy navigation.

* **Contact Form**:
  * Fields for full name, email, and phone number.
  * Dropdown for subject selection.
  * Message box and urgency flag.
  * Preferred contact method (radio buttons).

* **Location & Office Hours**:
  * Clearly formatted address block.
  * Tabular display of working days and hours.

* **Footer Section**:
* Includes copyright and links to privacy policy and terms.

##  🛠 Technologies Used
* **HTML5**: For semantic structure and content organization.
